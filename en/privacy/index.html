Privacy Policy

Globalmart Inc.

This is an English translation of our Korean privacy policy. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails. For questions, contact gvmart@hanmail.net.

Globalmart Inc. (“the Company”) publishes this policy under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act to protect your personal data and to handle related concerns promptly.

1. Personal data we collect and how

  • Consultation requests: name, company, email, phone number, service of interest, target region and message.
  • You enter this information yourself in the inquiry form on this website. The website has no member sign-up.
  • We do not set our own cookies or collect visit logs on this website. The external services listed in section 7 may, however, receive your IP address and browser information when a page loads.

2. Purpose of use

  • Receiving and answering consultation requests about overseas market entry
  • Contacting you about our services

3. Retention period

  • Consultation requests are kept for one year after the consultation and then deleted.
  • Where a law requires longer retention, we keep the data for the period that law sets.

4. Your right to refuse

  • You may refuse to consent to the collection and use of your personal data.
  • If you refuse, you cannot use the online inquiry form, but you can still contact us by phone (+82-2-499-7977) or email (gvmart@hanmail.net).

5. Provision to third parties

  • We do not give your personal data to third parties without your consent, except where a law provides otherwise.

6. Processors and transfer outside Korea

  • Form delivery: FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) forwards what you enter to our email inbox. We keep received requests as email.
  • Recipient abroad: FormSubmit (contact: formsubmit.co website) · Country: outside Korea (where the FormSubmit service servers are located)
  • Items transferred: name, company, email, phone number, service of interest, target region and message
  • When and how: when you submit the inquiry form, sent over the internet (HTTPS encrypted)
  • Purpose and retention: to deliver your request to our email (gvmart@hanmail.net); retention at FormSubmit follows that service's own policy.
  • If you do not want your data transferred abroad, please contact us directly by phone (+82-2-499-7977) or email (gvmart@hanmail.net) instead of using the form.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the website, connection data such as your IP address may be recorded on GitHub's servers.
  • If our processors or their tasks change, we will announce it in this policy.

7. External services on this website

  • Photos: Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) · Fonts: jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) · Map: Google Maps (Location page)
  • These services may receive your IP address, browser type and the address of the page you visit when a page loads, and Google Maps may use cookies. They process this information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or keep it.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website still works without cookies, and you can use the Naver, Kakao or Google Maps links instead of the embedded map.

8. Your rights

  • You may ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data or to stop its processing. Contact us using the details below and we will act without delay.
  • You may also make these requests through a legal representative or a person you authorise.

9. Destruction

  • When the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved, we destroy the data without delay so that it cannot be recovered.

10. Security measures

  • Access is limited to the staff in charge, and data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

11. Privacy officer

  • Name: Ha-hee Choi
  • Phone: +82-2-499-7977
  • Email: gvmart@hanmail.net

12. Remedies

  • For reports of or advice on privacy infringement, you can contact the following Korean bodies.
  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center (KISA): 118 (in Korea) · privacy.kisa.or.kr
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: 1833-6972 · www.kopico.go.kr
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office: 1301 (in Korea) · www.spo.go.kr
  • Korean National Police Agency: 182 (in Korea) · ecrm.police.go.kr

13. Effective date

  • This policy takes effect on the day the website is published.
  • If this policy changes, we will announce it on this website before the change takes effect.
