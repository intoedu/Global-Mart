Hello, and thank you for visiting Globalmart.

The internet has made the world faster and smaller. Business can no longer stay within one country; it has to aim for the global market. Globalmart is committed to helping companies make that move.

We carry out overseas branch work on behalf of Korean companies. To do this we work with the International Region Development Cooperation Association (IRDCA), which trains overseas market development specialists, and we place those specialists abroad.

First, we want to meet Korean companies that are looking to market globally. We can run market development activities in many countries at the same time.

We also work with overseas companies seeking partnerships with Korean firms or entry into the Korean market. We focus in particular on taking Korean technology, especially Korean software, to the world.

Through this work, we will do our best for the globalization of both Korean and overseas companies. Thank you.
