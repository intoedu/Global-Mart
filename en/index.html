Blockchain (Mainnet)

Blockchain is a distributed ledger that protects data from tampering and hacking. Financial institutions, logistics companies, card issuers and governments handle huge volumes of data and are exposed to attacks; recording the ledger in a distributed way, the 'mainnet', protects them.
